How we match dogs into groups

When your dog comes to Complete Canine for daycare, he or she will be integrated into the supervised play group that best suits him. This is done carefully and at a pace within each dog's comfort level.

  • Groups are determined by factors such as size and temperament.
  • Introductions happen gradually, at each dog's own pace.
  • Some dogs may go out alone if that works better for them.
  • Your dog will also have rest time during the day.
