Boarding

Complete Canine is a place for dogs to have safe and loving care while you can’t be with them. We are very proud of our service and client satisfaction as pet care providers. The safety, contentment, and welfare of your pet is as important to us as it is to you!

  • Radiant floors in winter and air-conditioning in summer.
  • Each dog kennel has its own in and out run.
  • Staff on premises 24 hours a day.
  • Multiple fenced areas allowing dogs outdoor playtime with other dogs when appropriate and in carefully selected groups. Some dogs may go out alone if that is best for them.
  • You can bring your own food to avoid digestive upsets or we will feed your pet food we have here at the facility.
  • We provide beds or blankets for everyone. You may bring your own so your pup can have something that smells like home.
  • We’ll give medications at no charge.
  • Please inform us if your pet has any special needs or allergies. They must be able to walk and not be incontinent.
  • Boarding reservations are appreciated.
  • Pets must be current on Rabies, Distemper and Kennel Cough vaccines, flea free and parasite free.
  • Pet will be bathed at owner's expense if not flea-free.
