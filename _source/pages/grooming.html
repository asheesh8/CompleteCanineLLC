Dog & Cat Grooming

Bathing, nail trimming and experienced full service grooming for all breeds.

What you can expect...

  • An experienced professional groomer
  • The best quality in grooming products and shampoos
  • Grooming at reasonable prices

Grooming includes:

  • Bathing
  • Brushing and combing
  • Nails clipped
  • Ears cleaned
  • Blow dried
  • Trimmed and shaped
  • Styling

Additional services:

  • Medicated Shampoos
  • Flea treatments
  • Dematting (if too matted, clipping may be necessary for the comfort and safety of your pet)
  • Deskunking

Pets must be current on Rabies, Distemper and Kennel Cough vaccines.
