Dog Daycare

Complete Canine's Dog Daycare is a place for dogs to have fun in a safe and caring environment while you can't be with them.

What you can expect...

When your dog comes to Complete Canine for daycare, he or she will be integrated into the supervised play group that best suits him. This is done carefully and at a pace within each dog’s comfort level. Groups are determined based on factors such as size and temperament. Your dog will also have rest time during the day.

  • A full day of exercise and play
  • Attention from caring staff
  • Interaction with other dogs to build positive social behavior and alleviation of boredom by playing with other dogs. Some dogs may go out alone if that works better for them.
  • Good behavior and manners are promoted.
  • Peace of mind that your dog is safe and happy.
  • Please inform us of any allergies or special needs.

Pets must be current on Rabies, Distemper and Kennel Cough vaccines.
